Every Script Follows the Same 8 Steps

Proven, consistent, and designed to capture every lead — regardless of industry.

1

Greeting

Time-aware. Adapts automatically for open hours, before opening, and after closing.

2

Service Type

Industry-specific question to identify exactly what the caller needs.

3

Description

Capture a brief summary of the job or problem in the caller's own words.

4

Contact Info

Name and phone number (mandatory). Email is collected but always optional.

5

Address

Confirm the service location or neighbourhood for the job.

6

Quotes

Only if asked. Rough range only — never confirm exact pricing.

7

Confirmation

Read back the details and confirm exactly when the callback will happen.

8

Rules

Built-in guardrails to keep every call on track and on brand.
