## How to Find Email Addresses

Most small contractors don't publish emails. Use these methods:

1. **Contact form** — Submit the form and ask them to reply with the best email to reach them
2. **Call directly** — "Hi, I wanted to send over some information about a tool that could help your business — what's the best email to reach you or the owner?"
3. **Hunter.io** — Enter their domain (e.g. potvinplumbing.com) to find any indexed emails (free tier: 25/mo)
4. **LinkedIn** — Search business name + owner name, often have email in profile
5. **Yelp / Google Business** — Some listings include email
