Pros and cons of switching to AI

✅

AI Receptionist — Pros

  • Answers every call in under 2 seconds, every time
  • Books appointments in real time — no message queue
  • 60–80% lower monthly cost
  • Integrates directly with scheduling software
  • Consistent, accurate, never has a bad day
  • 24/7 included, no premium after-hours rate
⚠️

AI Receptionist — Cons

  • Handles scripted conversations better than unscripted
  • A small percentage of callers prefer human interaction
  • Requires 72-hour setup
  • Complex, emotional calls still need human follow-up
✅

Live Answering Service — Pros

  • Human voice for callers who strongly prefer it
  • Can handle highly unscripted conversations
  • No tech setup or integration required
❌

Live Answering Service — Cons

  • Costs 2–4x more per month
  • Business hours only without premium pricing
  • Cannot book appointments — only takes messages
  • Message accuracy varies by operator
  • 1–2 week setup time
