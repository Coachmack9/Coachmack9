How ProBot Handles After-Hours Calls

Six steps. Under 3 minutes. Caller gets help — you get the job.

📞

Step 1: Instant Answer

The AI answers in under 2 seconds with your business name and a friendly greeting. No hold music. No "please leave a message." A real conversation starts immediately.

🔍

Step 2: Issue Identification

The AI asks what's happening and listens for your emergency keywords — burst pipe, no heat, gas smell, flooding, sparking — anything you define during setup.

🚨

Step 3: Emergency Escalation

Emergency detected? The AI reassures the caller help is on the way, collects their name and address, then immediately texts your on-call technician with the full job summary.

📅

Step 4: Non-Emergency Booking

Non-emergency calls get the full appointment booking flow — the AI describes available slots, confirms a time, and books directly into ServiceTitan, Jobber, or Housecall Pro.

📱

Step 5: Caller Confirmation

Every caller receives a text confirmation — either "help is on the way" for emergencies or a booking confirmation with appointment details for scheduled jobs.

📋

Step 6: Morning Summary

You wake up to a complete log of every call — who called, what they needed, what action was taken, and which jobs are now on your schedule. Nothing falls through the cracks.
