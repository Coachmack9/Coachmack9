Questions, Answered.

How fast can I go live?

48 hours from signed contract to live calls. Most contractors are recovering missed jobs within 2 days.

Do my callers know it's AI?

Most don't notice. The AI sounds natural and conversational — trained specifically on contractor vocabulary and scenarios. When callers ask directly, it's transparent. But they usually just appreciate getting an answer at 11 PM instead of voicemail.

What happens with emergency calls?

The AI detects emergency keywords like "burst pipe," "no heat," "sparks," and texts you immediately so you can pick up live. Non-emergencies get booked directly into your calendar.

Does it work with my existing phone system?

Yes. We integrate with Google Voice, RingCentral, existing VoIP lines, and your cell number. Keep your current phone. Keep your current number. No rip-and-replace.
