The problem every contractor has

Your phone rings while you're under a sink, on a roof, or pulling wire in an attic. You can't answer. The caller hits voicemail, hangs up, and dials the next company on Google.

You just lost a job worth $300 to $8,000 — and you'll never know it happened.

The math gets worse when you add up nights, weekends, and the 15+ minutes between jobs. For most contractors in the Greater Boston area, missed calls cost more per year than a full-time employee.

The industry data:

  • Many callers hang up on voicemail instead of leaving a message

  • Each missed call is worth $200-$2,000 in potential revenue

What ProBot's AI receptionist actually does

1. Answers every call — instantly

The AI picks up within two rings, 24 hours a day, 7 days a week. No voicemail. No hold music. No "please press 1."

2. Qualifies the lead

It collects everything you'd want a human receptionist to gather: caller's name and phone number, type of service needed, urgency (emergency vs. scheduled), address and service area, preferred appointment window.

3. Books the appointment

If the job qualifies, the AI checks your calendar and books the slot directly — confirmed with the caller while they're still on the line.

4. Routes emergencies to you in real time

If a caller says "no heat" or "water everywhere," the AI texts you immediately with the full details so you can decide to pick up live.

5. Follows up automatically

If the caller isn't ready to book, the AI enters them into a multi-day follow-up sequence over SMS. Some of these "almost" leads turn into booked jobs within a week.
