How Much Does an AI Receptionist Cost for a Contractor?

Direct answer

An AI receptionist for a contractor costs $297 to $897 per month plus a one-time setup fee of $497 to $997. ProBot Solutions offers three packages: Starter ($297/mo + $497 setup) with 2 AI systems, Growth ($597/mo + $797 setup) with 4 systems, and Full Stack ($897/mo + $997 setup) with all 5 systems. Every package is month-to-month with no long-term contracts, and all are backed by a guarantee: 3 booked jobs in your first 30 days or you don't pay the monthly fee. At these prices, 1-2 additional booked jobs would cover the monthly cost.

$297/month + $497 one-time setup

Includes:

  • AI Receptionist (24/7 call answering, lead qualification, direct calendar booking)
  • Missed Call Text-Back (60-second automated text response to missed calls)

Best for: Solo operators or single-truck shops focused on capturing every call.

$597/month + $797 one-time setup

Everything in Starter, plus:

  • Reactivate Old Leads (AI outreach to dormant leads in your CRM)

Full Stack — All 5 AI Systems

$897/month + $997 one-time setup

Everything in Growth, plus:

  • Automated Lead Gen, Email & Appointment Scheduling (full funnel automation from web form to booking)

Best for: Contractors who want end-to-end automation — every inbound call, every missed call, every old lead, every review, and every new lead captured and nurtured automatically.

How it compares to alternatives

Hiring a full-time receptionist

  • Greater Boston salary range: $45,000-$60,000/year
  • Plus benefits, payroll taxes, PTO, training
  • Fully loaded cost: $55,000-$75,000/year
  • Works 40 hours/week max — not 24/7

Annual cost comparison:

  • ProBot Starter: $3,564/year (plus $497 one-time)
  • ProBot Full Stack: $10,764/year (plus $997 one-time)
  • Full-time receptionist: $55,000-$75,000/year

Traditional answering service

  • $500-$1,500/month for limited-hour coverage
  • Takes messages, doesn't book jobs
  • Usually no CRM or calendar integration
  • Human-paced response times

Voicemail (free)

  • Many callers hang up without leaving a message
  • Many callers go to the next contractor
  • Lost revenue depends on your trade and call volume

Doing nothing

  • Depends on missed call rate and average job value
  • Typical contractor: $50,000-$150,000/year in lost revenue from missed calls

Setup fees explained

The one-time setup fee covers:

  • Custom script writing tailored to your trade and service area
  • Phone system integration (Google Voice, RingCentral, VoIP, or cell)
  • Calendar integration (Google, Outlook, Jobber, Housecall Pro, ServiceTitan)
  • CRM connection if needed
  • Test calls, tuning, and live-launch support

Setup fees scale with package because bigger packages require more integrations and workflow builds.
